How to read this

Seat data pulled from BA's own Reward Flight Finder (logged in to Heni's account) on 18 Oct 2026, 11:38, searching London (any airport) for 2 adults in economy. A weekend shows only if BA had reward seats for two on the outbound day and the return day. It means at least one direct BA flight that day had seats, not that every time slot does, so check flight times before you get attached. Reward seats move daily, so book soon once you pick.

Avios for two = one return reward price (the voucher makes the second seat free), off-peak to peak, from Head for Points' published BA chart. Cash is the per-person Reward Flight Saver fee from the same chart; with a Companion Voucher BA can show different cash and Avios combinations at checkout, so treat it as indicative. Long-haul return for one is 55,000 to 66,000 Avios, which is more than the 47,447 currently in the account unless you top up or pick a lower-Avios, higher-cash option.
